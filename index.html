Install

Two minutes and you're done

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Yandex Browser and other Chromium-based browsers. After installing, a welcome page shows you where to click.

Prefer to install manually? Follow the steps below.

  1. Download the archive and unzip it to any folder.
  2. Open the extensions page: chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the focus-cams folder.
  5. A welcome page opens — it walks you through pinning the icon and a 1-minute demo.

FAQ

Questions

Does it block every site?

No, only the ones on your list. By default that's social and entertainment sites (Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, Facebook, Twitch, Pinterest), and you can change the list completely.

Why does the browser ask for access to all sites?

Otherwise the extension couldn't notice that you're opening a distracting site. It doesn't read page content and sends nothing anywhere.

Can I stop focus early?

Yes — click the mountain icon and press “Stop focus”.

A cam didn't load — what now?

Open the distracting site again: a new cam is picked each time. You can remove a broken cam in the settings.

Privacy policy

Focus: Nature Cams does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your site list, cam list and timer state are kept only in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage) and sync only through your own browser account if sync is enabled. The extension uses no analytics, ads or external servers. Webcams open on their owners' websites (SkylineWebcams, HDOnTap and others), which have their own policies.
